Our story

A.T. stands for Ambling Turtle.

A.T. Glace is the dessert spin-off of Ambling Turtle café. We took what people loved there and gave it its own home at Dairy Farm Mall: gelato made in small batches, waffles pressed to order, and teas brewed fresh.

Take it slow. Grab a scoop after a Dairy Farm hike, or a boba on your way home from Hillview.
