Our story

A.T. stands for Ambling Turtle.

A.T. Glace is the dessert spin-off of Ambling Turtle café. We took what people loved there and gave it its own home at Dairy Farm Mall: gelato made in small batches, waffles pressed to order, and teas brewed fresh.

Take it slow. Grab a scoop after a Dairy Farm hike, or a boba on your way home from Hillview.

Mon – Thu

Weekday hideout

Work, study, create.
Slowly.

On weekday afternoons the café is calm, bright and green. Bring your laptop, your notes or your camera and stay a while, with a hojicha latte or a scoop when you need a break.

  • Study sessionsWindow seats with daylight and trees outside.
  • Remote workSofas and small tables for a quieter afternoon.
  • Content creationPastel scoops, cone lamps and leafy backdrops that look great on camera.
  • Small catch-upsStudy groups, creative meetups and project chats.

Weekdays only (Mon – Thu, 1 – 9:30 PM; we're closed Fridays). Weekends belong to families and the play corner. Please be kind to other guests and order something every couple of hours. Wi-Fi and power points: to be confirmed by the café.
